# Requesting colleague perspectives

Former colleagues can add voluntary firsthand context to the
[public attestation thread](https://github.com/johnapaz/confluence-automation-toolkit-sample/issues/2).
They should write their own statements and speak only in a personal capacity.

## Suggested outreach message

Hi — I'm preparing a portfolio case study about the Confluence migration automation tools I built
and maintained while we worked together. I created a sanitized public reconstruction because the
original source contained internal details.

Would you be comfortable leaving a brief comment about your firsthand experience with the original
tools? It could mention which workflow you used, what manual work it replaced, whether the prompts
or documentation made it easier to use, and any effect you personally observed on time or
consistency.

Please use only your own words, speak in your personal capacity, and leave out internal URLs, data,
identifiers, architecture, or anything confidential. There is absolutely no obligation to comment.

Thread: https://github.com/johnapaz/confluence-automation-toolkit-sample/issues/2

## Consent and attribution

Before quoting a comment elsewhere in a portfolio, confirm that the author permits reuse of:

- their exact words;
- their name;
- their role at the time; and
- a link to their GitHub or LinkedIn profile.

Do not edit a colleague's statement in a way that changes its meaning. If grammar or length needs
adjustment, obtain approval for the final quotation.
